# Office for National Statistics (ONS)

Population estimates and projection under different scenarios can be downloaded from
[Population estimates portal](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/populationestimates).

### Available datasets

-**UK_ONS_MYE24UK** 2024 national and subnational mid-year population estimates
-**UK_ONS** Population projection under different migration and mortality scenarios
